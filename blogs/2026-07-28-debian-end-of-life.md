TL;DR | Go Straight to the Debian End of Life Audit Debian version 11 “Bullseye” (LTS) will be going end-of-life on the 31st of August. Once end of life, this version will no longer receive any security updates nor support from the Debian project. Debian is a free, open source operating system built on the Linux kernel and is widely used in enterprise environments.
