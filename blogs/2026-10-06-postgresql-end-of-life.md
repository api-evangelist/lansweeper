TL;DR Go straight to the PostgreSQL End-of-Life Audit PostgreSQL version 14 is going end-of-life on the 12th of November, 2026. The popular open-source object-relational database system is ending support for version 14 after just over 5 years. We have added a report to your Lansweeper installation to help you find out of date installations that are in need of an update.
