Pro Tips #84 Derived fields have actually been in the product for a couple of months now since our Connect 2026 release but they haven’t been highlighted properly yet by me. Today I’ll go over what they are, where you can find them and what they can actually offer, from basic Yes/No fields to complex calculations. For those who have been with Lansweeper for longer, you’ll know that many of our older more complex reports used the SQL Case statement.
