Modern IT teams are under tremendous pressure to fix vulnerabilities immediately, almost as soon as Security teams find them. Attackers have become much more adept at exploiting weaknesses quickly, so there’s no time to waste. The expectation is that IT will apply a patch and eliminate the risk, now.
